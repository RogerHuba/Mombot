     Farms sectors marked with BUBBLE parameters.  
            
     Farm Options: 
              [fig] - will strip fighters off planets
                [f] - will strip fuel off planets
                [o] - will strip organics off planets
                [e] - will strip equipment off planets
             [cash] - will grab cash off planets
          [reverse] - travels farm sectors in reverse
             [warp] - will warp planets to sell product
           [shield] - will ensure 200 Shields on shielded planets
             [colo] - will colonize planets with avaliable fuel
             [coln] - will balance colonists on planets
          [upgrade] - will upgrade all planets 
             [port] - will create and upgrade ports
            [build] - will create and upgrade planets and port
     [destroyports] - will destroy all non-class 3 ports during build
        [oneplanet] - only pops planet where none exist during build
          [nostrip] - won't attempt to strip during build
        [noupgrade] - won't attempt to upgrade during build
          [defense] - set cannons
           [amtrak] - uses amtrak sectors as farm sectors
       [allplanets] - uses tl sectors as farm sectors
          [movefig] - moves all fighters form planet to sector
              [off] - turns off farming script
            
     Data Options:
       {set} or {setdoor) plus [sector number]
                    - Marks sector as a Bubble Sector / Door
       {remove} plus [sector number]
                    - Removes marked sector
