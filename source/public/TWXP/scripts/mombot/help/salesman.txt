SALESMAN - Visits all ports in grid and buys fuel 
           and sells/buys organics and equipment.

   salesman [min port product] ({neg}otiate OR {hold}byhold) {skipcim} {upgradefuel} {nohaggle}
  
   {neg/hold}    Determines planet negotiate or hold selling approach
   {skipcim}     Does not run cim before starting route
   {upgradefuel} Upgrades fuel ports selling fuel
   {nohaggle}    Doesn't haggle when buying organics or equipment to keep from gaining exp
