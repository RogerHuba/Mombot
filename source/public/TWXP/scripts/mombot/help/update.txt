      update {figs} {limps} {armids} {cim} {warps}
        
          Checks deployment lists and sets sector
          parameters.  Shows differences since last
          update
          
          {figs} - fighter refresh
         {limps} - limpet refresh, including active
        {armids} - armid refresh
           {cim} - will refresh port report
           - optional: [upgrade level]
         {warps} - will refresh warp info
                                                 
          Examples:
                 >update figs limps armids
                 >update figs cim warps
                 >update all
                 >update cim warps
                 >figs
                 >limps
                 >cim 10000
