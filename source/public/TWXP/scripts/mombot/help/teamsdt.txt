                     
   
                <<<< teamsdt >>>>
   
     Steal, Dump, Transport with multiple bots 
     Options: 
              [ship1] - ship id of first cashing ship 
              [ship2] - ship id of second cashing ship 
              [ship3] - ship id of third cashing ship 
            {planet1} - planet id of first planet
            {planet2} - planet id of second planet
            {planet3} - planet id of third planet
           {override} - won't stop if blue not fedsafe
         {planetfuel} - will grab fuel from red planets
            {twoship} - will use two ships to cash
     Usage: 
             >teamsdt 3 4 5
             >teamsdt 3 4 5 11 12 13
             >teamsdt 6 7 4 override
     Note: 
            Planet ids are only necessary when multiple planets exist
            in sector or planet scanners are on ships.
